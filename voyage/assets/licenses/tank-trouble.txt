Tank Trouble

The game rules, graphics, the Laika character and the sound effects come
from Tank Trouble 2 (Flash version 4.0, 2007-2013) by Mads Purup,
www.purup.com / tanktrouble.com. The game logic, including the Laika
computer opponent, was translated from the ActionScript 2 of the original
TankTrouble_v4.0.swf and its external assets GameTank.swf, Tank.swf,
Crate.swf and Laika.swf. The graphics and sounds are the original assets
exported from those files. All rights in the game and its assets belong to
Mads Purup.

The Eurostile glyphs used for the score display are rendered from the font
embedded in the original game.

The built-in "other players' mazes" are mazes made by Tank Trouble players
with the original site's maze editor, as served by tanktrouble.com's
loadMaze.php and preserved by the Internet Archive's Wayback Machine. Titles
and creator names are shown as they were published.
