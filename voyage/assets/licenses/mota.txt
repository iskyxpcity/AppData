Magic Tower 50 (魔塔)

The floor maps, monster/item/NPC/event data, game rules (scripts/game_core.gd,
ported to Kotlin in mota/core), the classic pixel sprites and the sound effects
come from MagicTowerPlus (https://github.com/RainbowLand6/MagicTowerPlus,
commit 93f7fcb2f1608043c1a779f0c8bfa0087ec0529d). Its map/database data,
sprites and sound effects were in turn imported from tacthgin/MagicTower
(https://github.com/tacthgin/MagicTower). The background music tracks
(tower_theme.ogg, tower_boss.ogg) are original works of MagicTowerPlus.
Both projects are released under the MIT License, reproduced below.
